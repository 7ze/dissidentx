We live within an architecture of carefully maintained illusions. What passes
for public discourse is largely a choreographed performance, designed to
    substitute sentiment for thought and conformity for conviction.

The ancient fathers spoke of *nepsis*—watchfulness of the heart. Without this
vigilant attention, the mind absorbs every false image presented by the world,
mistaking current opinion for timeless truth. To stand against the consensus of
illusion requires unceasing discernment and the courage to remain grounded in
reality.
