## Where we are now

> There was once a dream, that was Rome.

Rome was once the seat of the Civilized World as we know it. But like all Great
things that rise and fall, Rome too fell - A sad tale of a civilization that
dared to stand tall as it did; From a Republic to an Empire that stretched
across the face of Earth. The Western World today, sits tied with a similar
fate, unless something changes.

In a culture of conformity and political correctness, I built this space for
those who still care about the Truth; For minds drawn to history, civilization,
philosophy, culture and Truth above all - unfiltered, unapologetic.

Less than a century ago, brave young Men landed on the shores of Normandy on
Doomsday, to fight an existential threat in the form of a combined Force of
Evil Totalitarianism to defend all that they believed in; As years have come to
pass, we seem to have forgotten the price of Liberty - We have grown
complacent; The same forces of Evil are knocking on our doors and we are far
too willingly, opening the doors and welcoming it with open arms. It is up to
us to keep the Vigil; Why, it is our solemn Duty, for the next generations, to
carry the torch of the Civilization forward.


## What's next

My mission is to embolden other young men, and in doing so build this platform for
the free minds to publish their works and writings. I see far too many young
men - Men with potential, vigor, passion - starved for purpose, distressed,
afraid to call out bullshit as they see it, who conform to what is set as the
norm without a question.

Many of you will remember this quote quite fondly; It fits quite nicely here
albeit being a bit dramatic -

> We’re the middle children of history, man. No purpose or place. We have no
Great War. No Great Depression. Our Great War’s a spiritual war… our Great
Depression is our lives.
